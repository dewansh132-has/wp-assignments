BLEACH

Bleach is a Japanese shōnen anime and manga series created by Tite Kubo. It follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a high school student who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper (Shinigami) after a Hollow attacks his family. As a substitute Soul Reaper, Ichigo defends humans from malevolent spirits called Hollows, guides lost souls to the afterlife (Soul Society), and maintains balance between the living world, the spirit realm, and Hueco Mundo—the desolate domain of Hollows.
